Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026 (Draft — Pending final legal review)

1. What we collect

PDF24x7 is an offline-first application designed to process all PDF documents locally on your device. We do not inspect, collect, or store your documents, images, or scan contents on external servers. If you choose to create an account for upcoming cloud synchronization, your account email and authentication records are securely managed through our authentication provider. Advertising and third-party tracking remain disabled until an ad provider is explicitly configured.

2. Where your files live

Every tool runs entirely on this device. Saved PDFs are kept in the app's own private storage, which other apps cannot read. They are never sent to external servers unless you explicitly configure cloud backup.

3. Google Drive backup

Off unless you turn it on. When enabled, the app signs in to the Google account you choose and copies your saved PDFs to your own Google Drive. Those files travel from this phone to Google directly. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which revokes the app's access to your account. What Google does with files in your Drive is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

4. Permissions

Camera is used only while you are scanning a document. Photos/Files access is used only when you pick an image or document to turn into a PDF or place a signature. Neither is used in the background, and the app asks the first time it needs each one.

5. Reading text from scans

Making a scanned PDF searchable uses a text recognition model built into the app. The page images and the text read from them never leave the device, and it requires no internet connection.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone of any age.

7. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the app will display it to you again before you carry on.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: bhingesamarth@gmail.com

2. Your files are your responsibility

The app changes documents you choose, and some changes cannot be undone: redaction removes content permanently, a password locks a file, cropping discards part of a page. Keep your own backup copy of anything you cannot afford to lose. We have no copy and cannot recover one for you.
